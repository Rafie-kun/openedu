# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This project deals with people's education and money, often under stress. Someone asking a
basic question about IB grades is not wasting your time.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith, especially from students who are using the platform in a second language.
- Criticise the code, the data or the decision, not the person.
- Respect privacy. Do not paste someone's profile, transcript, email or application details into an
  issue, a pull request or a chat.
- Accept a maintainer saying "we need a source for that" without turning it into an argument.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, slurs or sexual attention of any kind.
- Discriminatory comments about nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender, disability, age or
  education system.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues or reviews.
- Pressuring contributors to add unverified data, or to remove a verification warning.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
if the report is sensitive. Otherwise open an issue and tag a maintainer. Maintainers may edit or
remove comments, close threads, or block accounts. Decisions are explained to the people involved,
though not always in public.

## Attribution
